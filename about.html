Our Story

Founded in 2026, KAPETARA started with a simple goal: to serve exceptional specialty coffee to our local neighborhood. We focus on ethical direct-trade sourcing to support independent coffee growers globally.

Whether you're looking for a quiet place to get some work done or catching up with old friends, our warm atmosphere and friendly team are here to make you feel right at home.
